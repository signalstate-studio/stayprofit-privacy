Privacy Policy

Property planning with privacy in mind.

Effective date: 9 November 2026
App: StayProfit (iPhone and iPad)
Developer and controller: Marco De Ritis (Signal State Studio)
Location: Germany
Privacy and support contact: signalstatestudio@gmail.com

This policy describes how the current StayProfit app handles information. StayProfit is a rental profitability planning tool, not tax, legal, accounting, or investment advice.

1. Information you enter into StayProfit

StayProfit lets you enter property names and locations, nightly rates, occupancy estimates, booking channel allocations and fees, operating expenses, tax assumptions, saved scenarios, and preferences. The current app stores this information locally on your iPhone or iPad. It does not require an account or send these property records to a StayProfit-operated server.

2. Why and how information is used

Information you enter is processed on your device to calculate rental profit estimates, display charts, compare properties, save scenarios, and create reports and backups. The app does not use this information for advertising or user profiling. Device-local processing is necessary to provide the functionality you request.

3. PDF reports and JSON backups

You can voluntarily export financial reports as PDF and backup files as JSON. If you choose to save or share these files using the iOS share sheet or Files app, the selected storage or recipient service may process the exported copy under its own terms. Exports may contain financial details, so please store and share them securely.

4. Accounts, advertising, analytics, and third parties

The app version reviewed for this policy does not include StayProfit user accounts, advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, a StayProfit-hosted backend, or automatic cloud sync. Apple independently processes information related to app distribution, downloads, purchases, and device services under its own policies. If a future release adds new services or data practices, this policy will be updated before or alongside that release.

5. Email support

If you email signalstatestudio@gmail.com, your email address, message, and any information you choose to include will be used to reply and manage your request. Messages are handled through Google's Gmail service. The legal basis, where the GDPR applies, is taking steps to respond to your request or our legitimate interest in providing support. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for support and applicable legal obligations, and may be deleted earlier on request where appropriate.

6. Retention and deletion

Property data and app preferences remain on your device until you change or delete them or remove the app. Some copies may remain in device backups according to your Apple settings. Uninstalling StayProfit does not delete PDF or JSON files you previously exported; you can remove these directly from the locations where you saved them. You may contact us about support correspondence using the email above.

7. Your data protection rights

Where applicable, you may have rights to request information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability regarding personal data we process. You can contact us at signalstatestudio@gmail.com. We normally cannot inspect or retrieve records that exist only on your device. You can also lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority in the EU or EEA.

8. Security

We do not intentionally transfer property records to a StayProfit server. Your device's security settings and the destinations you choose for exports affect how well your information is protected. No method of storage is completely risk-free.

9. This website

This privacy-policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as connection and access data, to operate and secure the website. The page itself does not embed analytics, advertising trackers, external fonts, or cookies set by StayProfit. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for information on its processing.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when features or data-handling practices change. We will post the updated text here and update the effective date.
